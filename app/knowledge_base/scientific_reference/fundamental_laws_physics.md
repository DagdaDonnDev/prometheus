# Fundamental Laws of Physics

Tier 1 anchor source. These are universal laws. They do not change with location,
era, or circumstance. Every derivation in Prometheus must trace back to text like this.

## Conservation of Energy

Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only converted from one form to another.
In a closed system the total energy stays constant.

    E_total = constant
    Work done W = F × d  (force × distance moved in the direction of the force)
    Kinetic energy KE = ½ m v²
    Gravitational potential energy PE = m g h
    Power P = E / t  (energy per unit time, measured in watts, 1 W = 1 J/s)

Every machine loses some energy as heat. Efficiency = useful energy out / energy in,
always less than 1 (less than 100%).

## Conservation of Mass

In any physical or chemical process inside a closed system, mass is neither created
nor destroyed. The mass of the products equals the mass of the reactants.

## Conservation of Charge

Electric charge cannot be created or destroyed. The total charge of an isolated
system stays constant. Charge flowing into any point must equal charge flowing out.

## Conservation of Momentum

In a system with no external force, total momentum stays constant.

    Momentum p = m v
    p_before = p_after

## Newton's Laws of Motion

First law: an object stays at rest, or moves at constant velocity, unless acted on
by a net external force.

Second law: the net force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration.

    F = m a
    Force in newtons (N), mass in kilograms (kg), acceleration in m/s².
    Weight W = m g, where g = 9.81 m/s² at Earth's surface.

Third law: for every force there is an equal and opposite force. If A pushes on B,
B pushes back on A with the same magnitude in the opposite direction.

## Static Equilibrium

An object at rest (or moving at constant velocity) has no net force and no net
turning moment acting on it.

    Sum of forces = 0   (in every direction)
    Sum of moments = 0  (about any point)
    Moment M = F × d    (force × perpendicular distance from the pivot)

## Hooke's Law

For an elastic material within its elastic limit, extension is proportional to the
applied force.

    F = k x
    k is the stiffness (spring constant) in N/m, x is the extension in metres.

Beyond the elastic limit, the material deforms permanently and Hooke's law no longer holds.

## Laws of Thermodynamics

Zeroth law: if A is in thermal equilibrium with C, and B is in equilibrium with C,
then A and B are in equilibrium with each other. This is why thermometers work.

First law: the change in internal energy of a system equals heat added minus work done
by the system. ΔU = Q − W. This is conservation of energy applied to heat.

Second law: heat flows on its own only from hotter to colder bodies. No heat engine
can convert all heat into work. Entropy of an isolated system never decreases.

Third law: absolute zero (0 K, −273.15 °C) cannot be reached in a finite number of steps.

## Heat Transfer

    Heat needed to change temperature: Q = m c ΔT
    c is specific heat capacity. Water: c = 4186 J/(kg·K).
    Latent heat of vaporisation of water: about 2,260,000 J/kg (2.26 MJ/kg).
    Latent heat of fusion of ice: about 334,000 J/kg.

Heat moves by conduction (through solids), convection (by moving fluid), and
radiation (infrared, needs no medium).

## Gravity

Every mass attracts every other mass.

    F = G m1 m2 / r²
    Near Earth's surface this simplifies to W = m g.

## Pressure

    Pressure P = F / A  (force per unit area, pascals, 1 Pa = 1 N/m²)
    Pressure in a fluid at depth h: P = ρ g h
    Atmospheric pressure at sea level ≈ 101,325 Pa.
