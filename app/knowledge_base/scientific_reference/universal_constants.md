# Universal Constants

Tier 1 anchor source. Values are exact where marked, otherwise rounded.

    Speed of light in vacuum        c   = 299,792,458 m/s (exact)
    Gravitational constant          G   = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²
    Standard gravity                g   = 9.80665 m/s² (exact; use 9.81)
    Avogadro's number               N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³ per mol (exact)
    Gas constant                    R   = 8.314 J/(mol·K)
    Elementary charge               e   = 1.602176634 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (exact)
    Faraday constant                F   = 96,485 C/mol
    Boltzmann constant              k   = 1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K (exact)
    Absolute zero                   0 K = −273.15 °C
    Standard atmosphere             1 atm = 101,325 Pa (exact)
    Specific heat of water          c   = 4186 J/(kg·K)
    Density of water (4 °C)         ρ   = 1000 kg/m³
    Density of air (sea level, 15 °C) ≈ 1.225 kg/m³
    Earth mean radius               R   = 6,371 km
    Earth axial tilt                ≈ 23.44°
    Speed of sound in air (20 °C)   ≈ 343 m/s
    Solar irradiance at ground, clear midday ≈ 1000 W/m²

## Useful Rules of Thumb

Water boils at 100 °C at sea level and about 1 °C lower for every 300 m of altitude.
Sound travels about 1 km in 3 seconds — count seconds from lightning to thunder and
divide by 3 to get the distance in kilometres.
