# Water Purification — Complete Field Guide
Source: Procedures Library (High Confidence — Multiple Field-Tested Methods)

## The Rule of Threes
You can survive 3 minutes without air, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food.
Water is always the first priority after immediate safety.

## Finding Water

### Priority Order for Water Sources
1. Collected rainwater (safest — drink immediately)
2. Running water (stream, river — always purify)
3. Still water (lake, pond — purify thoroughly)
4. Groundwater (dig for it — often cleaner than surface)
5. Plant sources (dew, plant stems, cactus)
6. Solar still (last resort — low yield but zero input)

### Signs of Water Nearby
- Animal trails at dawn and dusk converge toward water
- Green vegetation in otherwise dry landscape
- Insects (especially bees) — within 6km of water
- Birds flying low and straight — especially at dawn/dusk
- Valley bottoms — water flows downhill always
- Dry riverbeds — dig in the outer bend, water often 30-60cm down
- Morning dew — collect with cloth before 9am

### Digging for Groundwater
Select lowest ground visible. Dig in outer bend of dry streambed.
Water seeps in at 30-90cm in most regions.
Line hole with cloth to filter silt.
Allow to settle 1 hour before drinking.

## Purification Methods

### 1. Boiling (Most Reliable)
Kills: 100% of bacteria, viruses, parasites
Does not remove: chemicals, heavy metals, sediment

Method:
- Bring water to rolling boil (large bubbles, vigorous)
- At sea level: boil 1 minute
- Above 2000m altitude: boil 3 minutes (lower boiling point)
- Let cool in covered container
- Taste improves by pouring between containers to aerate

Improvised boiling containers:
- Any metal container (tin cans, salvaged pots)
- Clay pot (fired, not just dried — must be watertight)
- Large leaves folded and pinned (burn from outside — water prevents burning inside)
- Bark container (birch bark especially waterproof)
- Rock hollow — heat rocks in fire, drop into water

### 2. Ceramic Pot Filter (Best Long-Term Solution)

Removes: 99.9% bacteria, parasites, protozoa
Does NOT remove: viruses, dissolved chemicals
Combined with: boiling or chlorine for complete purification

Materials needed:
- Local clay (plastic when wet, cracks when dry — test with finger)
- Combustible material — sawdust, rice husks, or ground charcoal
- Colloidal silver solution (optional — vastly improves results)
- Two containers of any material for system

Mix ratio by dry weight:
- 50% clay
- 50% combustible material (sawdust preferred)
- Water gradually to workable dough consistency

Construction process:
1. Mix dry ingredients thoroughly before adding any water
2. Add water slowly — knead like bread dough for 20 minutes minimum
3. Let rest covered 24 hours (improves workability)
4. Form pot by hand — walls must be uniform 2-3cm thick throughout
5. Smooth all surfaces — cracks will cause failures
6. Dry SLOWLY — 2 to 4 weeks in shade, never in direct sun
   Fast drying causes cracking and failure
7. Fire in kiln or open hardwood fire:
   - Surround with hot coals gradually
   - Raise temperature slowly over 2 hours minimum
   - Peak temperature approximately 900°C — pots glow dull orange
   - Maintain peak 1 hour minimum
   - Let cool completely inside the fire — 12 hours minimum
   - Moving hot ceramics causes cracking
8. Test flow rate immediately:
   - Fill with clean water to the rim
   - Count drips over 1 hour
   - Target: 1 to 3 liters per hour
   - Too fast (over 3L/hr) = too porous = ineffective, remake
   - Too slow (under 0.5L/hr) = clogged or over-fired = try anyway

Silver treatment:
- Mix 1 teaspoon colloidal silver in 1 cup clean water
- Paint entire inner surface while pot is still slightly warm
- Allow to dry completely before use
- Improves bacterial removal from approximately 99% to 99.99%
- Silver prevents biofilm growth inside filter

System setup:
- Place ceramic filter pot in upper container
- Lower container catches filtered water
- Keep both covered at all times
- Never touch the inside of the filter with bare hands
- Clean outer surface only — never scrub inside

Maintenance:
- If flow rate slows: scrub outer surface gently with clean brush
- Never scrub inside — destroys filter structure
- Replace if cracked, dropped, or flow stops entirely
- Lifespan: 1-2 years of daily use

### 3. Bio-Sand Filter (Community Scale)

Removes: 99.9% bacteria, 98% viruses, 100% parasites
Build time: 1 day construction, 3-4 weeks to establish biological layer
Throughput: 400-800ml per hour (slow but continuous)

Materials:
- Container minimum 90cm deep (concrete most durable, barrel acceptable)
- Coarse gravel (pebbles 5-15mm diameter)
- Fine gravel (pebbles 2-5mm diameter)
- Clean fine sand (particle size 0.15-0.35mm — crucial)
- Outlet pipe or spout at bottom
- Lid to exclude light and debris

Construction:
1. Drill or create outlet hole 5cm from very bottom
2. Plug outlet during construction
3. Layer from bottom up:
   - 5cm coarse gravel
   - 5cm fine gravel
   - 50-60cm fine sand — this is the critical layer
   - Leave 5cm space above sand for standing water layer
4. Add diffuser plate on top of sand to prevent disturbance when filling
   (flat plate with holes, or simply a cloth over sand)
5. Fill slowly with water — outlet must flow continuously

The biological layer — the schmutzdecke:
This is the magic. It forms naturally in the top 2cm of sand.
A living biofilm of microorganisms that eat pathogens.
Takes 3-4 weeks of daily use to fully establish.
During establishment: also boil or use chemical treatment.
After establishment: the filter works by biology, not just physics.
NEVER scrub, bleach, or disturb the top layer.
If filter sits unused 2+ weeks: biological layer dies, must re-establish.
If you must stop using it: keep it wet, cover it, restart slowly.

Operation:
- Pour water slowly on diffuser plate daily
- Never let water level drop below sand surface (kills biological layer)
- Always keep covered — light kills the biological layer
- Filtered water exits through outlet into clean container
- Keep outlet covered when not filling

Cleaning (when flow rate drops significantly):
1. Remove top 2cm of sand — only the top 2cm
2. Wash this sand with clean water
3. Replace
4. Biological layer re-establishes in 1-2 weeks

Effectiveness vs source:
River water: excellent results
Muddy flood water: pre-settle before filtering
Sewage water: not suitable — will clog and overwhelm
Rainwater: excellent — barely needs filtering

### 4. Solar Disinfection (SODIS)

Removes: 99.9% bacteria, viruses, and parasites
Requires: Clear PET plastic bottles (or glass), direct sunlight
Does NOT work: on cloudy days, or on turbid (muddy) water

Method:
1. Fill clear PET plastic bottles completely (no air space)
2. Water must be clear — turbid water blocks UV penetration
   If turbid: filter through cloth first, allow to settle, decant
3. Place bottles on reflective surface if available (metal sheet, foil)
4. Expose to direct sunlight:
   - Full sun: 6 hours minimum
   - Partially cloudy: 2 days
   - Overcast: does not work — use another method
5. Drink directly from bottle or store sealed

SODIS effectiveness boosted by:
- Placing bottles on corrugated metal roof (heat + UV combined)
- Adding small amount of lemon juice (acidifies water, kills more pathogens)
- Using 2L bottles or smaller — larger bottles less effective

### 5. Chemical Treatment

Chlorine bleach (sodium hypochlorite):
- Use unscented household bleach 5-6% concentration
- Clear water: 2 drops per liter, wait 30 minutes
- Cloudy water: 4 drops per liter, wait 60 minutes
- Water should smell faintly of chlorine — if not, repeat
- Shake to oxygenate before drinking

Improvising chlorine:
- Pool shock (calcium hypochlorite 68-78%) is more stable for storage
- Dissolve 1/4 teaspoon pool shock in 2 liters water to make stock solution
- Add 1 part stock to 100 parts water to treat

Iodine (2% tincture of iodine):
- Clear water: 5 drops per liter, wait 30 minutes
- Cloudy water: 10 drops per liter, wait 60 minutes
- Do not use long-term — thyroid effects over months
- Do not use during pregnancy

Potassium permanganate:
- Available as purple crystals in most pharmacies
- Add crystals until water turns pale pink — not deep purple
- Wait 30 minutes
- Also treats skin infections and athlete's foot

### 6. Solar Still (Emergency Only)

Yield: 250-1000ml per day — very low, use multiple stills
Works: with any moisture source including urine, vegetation, soil
Does not require: any containers except plastic sheet

Construction:
1. Dig hole 90cm wide, 45cm deep in sunny location
2. Place collection container in center of hole (any container)
3. Place moisture sources around (not in) container:
   - Vegetation, leaves, plant material
   - Urine (distillation purifies it)
   - Brackish or salt water
   - Muddy water
4. Cover entire hole with clear plastic sheet
5. Weight edges with soil or rocks to seal completely
6. Place small rock in center of sheet directly above container
7. Sunlight heats air and moisture inside
8. Condensation forms on plastic, runs down to center, drips into container

Maximize yield:
- Dig deeper (more moisture from ground)
- Add more vegetation especially succulent plants
- Operate overnight too — temperature differential drives condensation
- Multiple stills working simultaneously essential

Drinking tube:
- Run plastic tube from container through soil to outside
- Sip without dismantling still — maintains temperature

## Water Safety Summary

| Method | Bacteria | Viruses | Parasites | Chemicals | Time Required |
|--------|----------|---------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Boiling | 100% | 100% | 100% | No | 1-3 minutes |
| Ceramic filter | 99.9% | Partial | 100% | No | Continuous |
| Bio-sand filter | 99.9% | 98% | 100% | No | 3-4 weeks setup |
| SODIS | 99.9% | 99% | 99% | No | 6 hours |
| Chlorine | 99.9% | 99.9% | Partial | No | 30 minutes |
| Solar still | 100% | 100% | 100% | Mostly | 6-8 hours |

For maximum safety in unknown water: filter first to remove particles, then boil.
Two-step treatment is always more effective than one step alone.
